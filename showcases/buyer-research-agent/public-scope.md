# Publication scope

Included: reconstructed UI, normalized evidence gates, SQLite queue, four
synthetic records, source code, tests and freshly captured screenshots.

Excluded: real companies and contacts, customer feedback, quotes and orders,
research prompts, full execution scripts, browser sessions, private source
files, original task reports, credentials and internal execution logs.

All evidence dates and domains are fixture values. Verification flags demonstrate
classifier inputs; they are not proof that a source was fetched. No private
research counts or claimed business uplift appear in the public UI.
