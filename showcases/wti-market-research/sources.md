# Source Register

The study used seven official public series. Raw files are not mirrored in this
repository; the links below point to the source pages used for the research.

| Series | Role | Institution | Frequency | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DCOILWTICO | WTI target price | U.S. Energy Information Administration via FRED | Daily | [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DCOILWTICO) |
| DCOILBRENTEU | Brent spread component | U.S. Energy Information Administration via FRED | Daily | [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DCOILBRENTEU) |
| WCESTUS1 | U.S. commercial crude inventory | U.S. Energy Information Administration | Weekly | [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?f=W&n=PET&s=WCESTUS1) |
| WCRFPUS2 | U.S. crude oil field production | U.S. Energy Information Administration | Weekly | [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?f=W&n=PET&s=WCRFPUS2) |
| DTWEXBGS | Broad U.S. dollar index | Federal Reserve Board via FRED | Daily | [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DTWEXBGS) |
| DFII10 | 10-year real Treasury yield | Federal Reserve Board via FRED | Daily | [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DFII10) |
| VIXCLS | VIX | Cboe via FRED | Daily | [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS) |

## Citation and revision notes

- The research snapshot was downloaded on 2026-08-20 and the common weekly sample ends on 2026-08-14.
- FRED series pages provide the series-specific citation and release information.
- EIA source data is public, but historical estimates can be revised.
- VIX is a Cboe index. The source and Cboe ownership should remain visible when the series is discussed.
- The package does not redistribute raw source files or claim that the snapshot is a point-in-time vintage database.
